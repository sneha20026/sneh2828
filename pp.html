Know More About PCOD & PCOS Disorder


PCOD (Polycystic Ovarian Disease) and PCOS (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome) are common hormonal disorders that affect women of reproductive age. These conditions can impact menstrual health, fertility, metabolism, and overall well-being. Although the terms are often used interchangeably, PCOS is generally considered a more complex hormonal and metabolic disorder than PCOD.

What is PCOD?

PCOD occurs when the ovaries produce immature or partially mature eggs, which can develop into cysts over time. The ovaries may become enlarged and produce excess male hormones (androgens), leading to hormonal imbalance.

What is PCOS?

PCOS is a hormonal disorder characterized by irregular ovulation, elevated androgen levels, and the presence of multiple small cysts in the ovaries. It is associated with insulin resistance, inflammation, and metabolic disturbances that can affect various body systems.

Common Symptoms of PCOD & PCOS


  • Irregular or missed menstrual periods

  • Excessive facial or body hair growth (hirsutism)

  • Acne and oily skin

  • Weight gain or difficulty losing weight

  • Hair thinning or hair loss

  • Darkening of skin, especially around the neck and underarms

  • Difficulty conceiving or infertility

  • Fatigue and low energy levels

Causes and Risk Factors


  • Hormonal imbalance

  • Insulin resistance

  • Genetic predisposition

  • Obesity or excess body weight

  • Chronic inflammation

  • Unhealthy dietary and lifestyle habits

Ayurvedic Treatment for PCOD & PCOS Disorder


In Ayurveda, PCOD (Polycystic Ovarian Disease) and PCOS (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome) are considered disorders resulting from an imbalance of the Kapha, Vata, and sometimes Pitta Doshas. Ayurvedic treatment aims to restore hormonal balance, improve metabolism, regulate menstrual cycles, and support overall reproductive health.

Ayurvedic Approach to PCOD & PCOS


Ayurveda focuses on treating the root cause rather than just managing symptoms. Treatment plans are individualized based on a person's body constitution (Prakriti) and the severity of the condition.

1. Herbal Remedies


Several Ayurvedic herbs are traditionally used to support hormonal and reproductive health:

  • Shatavari (Asparagus racemosus) – Supports female reproductive health and hormonal balance

  • Ashoka (Saraca indica) – Helps maintain healthy menstrual cycles.

  • Lodhra (Symplocos racemosa) – Traditionally used for gynecological wellness.

  • Guduchi (Tinospora cordifolia) – Supports immunity and metabolism.

  • Triphala – Aids digestion and detoxification.

  • Aloe Vera – May help support menstrual regularity and overall reproductive health.

2. Panchakarma Therapies


Ayurvedic detoxification therapies may be recommended under professional supervision:

  • Virechana (Therapeutic Purgation) – Helps eliminate excess Pitta and toxins.

  • Basti (Medicated Enema) – Balances Vata dosha and supports reproductive health.

  • Udvartana (Herbal Powder Massage) – Often used to support weight management and improve circulation.

  • Abhyanga (Oil Massage) – Promotes relaxation and balances doshas.

3. Dietary Recommendations


A healthy diet plays a vital role in managing PCOD and PCOS:

Recommended Foods

  • Include whole grains, legumes, and fiber-rich foods.

  • Choose healthy fats such as nuts and seeds.

  • Reduce refined sugar and processed foods.

  • Avoid excessive oily, fried, and junk foods.

  • Drink adequate water throughout the day.
