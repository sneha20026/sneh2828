Know More About Kidney Disease

Kidney disease affects the body's ability to filter waste and extra fluids from the blood properly. It can lead to swelling, weakness, fatigue, back pain, and frequent urination problems.

Pragati Ayurveda provides natural homemade Ayurvedic remedies prepared with pure herbs that help support kidney health and improve body wellness naturally.




What is kidney Disease?

Kidney disease is a condition in which the kidneys are damaged and cannot filter waste, excess water, and toxins from the blood properly.




What Do Kidneys Do?


The kidneys are two bean-shaped organs located on either side of the spine.Their functions:

  • Filtering waste products from the blood.

  • Removing extra water through urine.

  • Balancing minerals such as sodium and potassium.

  • Helping control blood pressure.

  • Producing hormones that support red blood cell production and bone health.

Types of Kidney Disease


1. Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD)


A long-term condition where kidney function gradually declines over months or years.

Common causes:

  • Diabetes

  • Hypertension

  • Kidney infections

  • Genetic disorders

2. Acute Kidney Injury (AKI)


A sudden loss of kidney function that develops within hours or days.

Common causes:

  • Severe dehydration

  • Serious infections

  • Certain medications

  • Reduced blood flow to the kidneys

3. Polycystic Kidney Disease (PKD)


An inherited disorder in which fluid-filled cysts grow inside the kidneys.

Symptoms of Kidney Disease:


Early kidney disease may cause no symptoms. As it progresses, symptoms may include:




  • Fatigue and weakness

  • Swelling in feet, ankles, or face

  • Changes in urination (more or less frequent)

  • Foamy or bloody urine

  • Nausea and vomiting

  • Loss of appetite

  • Muscle cramps

  • Itchy skin

4. Glomerulonephritis


Inflammation of the kidney's filtering units (glomeruli).

Common causes:

  • Blood or protein in urine

  • Swelling

  • High blood pressure.

5. Polycystic Kidney Disease (PKD)


An inherited disorder where multiple fluid-filled cysts develop in the kidneys.

Common causes:

  • Enlarged kidneys

  • High blood pressure

  • Back or side pain


Ayurvedic Concept and Management of Kidney Conditions


In Ayurveda, kidney disorders are not described exactly as modern medical diseases, but many kidney-related conditions are understood through concepts such as Mutravaha Srotas Dushti (disorders of the urinary system), Mutrakrichra (painful urination), Mutraghata (urinary obstruction), Ashmari (kidney/urinary stones), and fluid imbalance caused by disturbances of the Doshas—especially Vata, Pitta, and Kapha.




Preventing Aggravation of Kidney Conditions



1. Control Blood Pressure:

Maintaining blood pressure within a healthy range to reduce stress on the kidneys and prevent further kidney damage.

2. Manage Blood Sugar:

Keeping blood glucose levels under control, especially in people with diabetes, to protect the kidneys from damage caused by high sugar levels.

3. Stay Hydrated

Drinking an appropriate amount of water to help the kidneys remove waste products and maintain fluid balance in the body.

4. Avoid Smoking and Excess Alcohol

Refraining from tobacco use and limiting alcohol consumption to reduce kidney damage and improve overall health.

5. Exercise Regularly

Engaging in physical activity on a regular basis to improve blood circulation, control weight, and support kidney function.

6. Use Medicines Safely

Taking medications only as prescribed and avoiding unnecessary or excessive use of drugs that may damage the kidneys.

7. Prevent and Treat Infections Early

Recognizing and treating urinary tract and kidney infections promptly to prevent complications and kidney injury.


Diet and Lifestyle for Kidney Health


Diet and lifestyle for kidney health refer to healthy eating habits, proper hydration, regular physical activity, and other daily practices that help maintain normal kidney function, prevent kidney diseases, and slow the progression of existing kidney conditions.



1. Balanced Diet

Consuming a variety of nutritious foods that provide essential nutrients while reducing the burden on the kidneys.

2. Adequate Hydration

Drinking sufficient water to help the kidneys remove waste products and maintain fluid balance.

3. Control Protein Intake

Consuming appropriate amounts of protein because excessive protein may increase the workload of the kidneys, especially in people with kidney disease.

4. Increase Fruits and Vegetables

Including fresh fruits and vegetables in the diet to provide vitamins, minerals, antioxidants, and fiber.

5. Maintain Healthy Blood Sugar Levels

Choosing foods that help regulate blood glucose and reduce the risk of diabetic kidney damage.

Conclusion


Kidney conditions are disorders that affect the normal functioning of the kidneys, leading to problems in filtering waste products, maintaining fluid balance, and regulating essential body functions. Early diagnosis, proper medical treatment, a balanced diet, healthy lifestyle habits, and regular monitoring are important for preventing complications and slowing disease progression. From an Ayurvedic perspective, kidney disorders are associated with imbalances in the doshas and disturbances of the Mutravaha Srotas (urinary system). Ayurvedic management includes dietary regulation, lifestyle modifications, herbal remedies, Panchakarma therapies, yoga, and stress management to support overall kidney and urinary health.
