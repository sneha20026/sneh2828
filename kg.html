Know More About Kidney and Gallbladder Stones


Kidney Stones

Kidney stones are hard, crystal-like deposits that form inside the kidneys when minerals and salts in the urine become concentrated and stick together. These stones can vary in size from tiny grains to larger stones that may block the urinary tract.

Common Types of Kidney Stones

  • Calcium Stones – The most common type, usually made of calcium oxalate.

  • Uric Acid Stones –Often linked to dehydration and high-protein diets.

  • Struvite Stones – Usually develop after urinary tract infections.

  • Cystine Stones – Rare stones caused by a genetic disorder.


Symptoms of Kidney Stones


  • Severe pain in the back, side, or lower abdomen

  • Pain during urination

  • Blood in the urine

  • Frequent urge to urinate

  • Nausea and vomiting

  • Cloudy or foul-smelling urine

  • Fever and chills (if infection is present)

Cause of Kidney Stones


  • Inadequate water intake

  • Excessive salt consumption

  • High oxalate foods

  • Obesity

  • Family history

  • Certain medical conditions and medic

  • Fever and chills (if infection is present)

Gallbladder Stones

Gallstones are hardened deposits that form inside the gallbladder, a small organ located beneath the liver that stores bile used for digestion.

Types of Gallstones


1. Cholesterol Stones


  • Most common type.

  • Form when bile contains too much cholesterol.

2. Pigment Stones


  • Made from excess bilirubin..

  • More common in liver disease and certain blood disorders./li>

Symptoms of Gallstones


  • Sudden pain in the upper right abdomen

  • Pain after eating fatty foods

  • Indigestion and bloating

  • Pain between the shoulder blades

  • Nausea and vomiting

Cause of Kidney Stones


  • High cholesterol levels

  • Obesity

  • Rapid weight loss

  • Diabetes

  • Liver disorders

  • Poor dietary habits

  • Family history
