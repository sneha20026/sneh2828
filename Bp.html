Know More About Blood Pressure


Blood Pressure (BP) is the force exerted by circulating blood against the walls of the arteries. It is an important indicator of heart and blood vessel health. Blood pressure naturally rises and falls throughout the day, but consistently high or low readings can lead to health problems.

Types of Blood Pressure


1. Normal Blood Pressure


  • Indicates healthy heart and blood vessel function.

  • Usually around 120/80 mmHg or lower.

  • Associated with a lower risk of heart disease and stroke.

2. Elevated Blood Pressure


  • Blood pressure is higher than normal but not yet hypertension.

  • May progress to high blood pressure if not managed.

  • Lifestyle modifications are often recommended.

3. Hypertension


Stage 1 Hypertension

  • Systolic: 130–139 mmHg

  • Diastolic: 80–89 mmHg

Stage 2 Hypertension

  • Systolic: 140 mmHg or higher

  • Diastolic: Diastolic: 90 mmHg or higher

4. Hypertensive Crisis


  • Blood pressure exceeds 180/120 mmHg.

  • Requires immediate medical attention.

  • May lead to stroke, heart attack, or organ damage.

Common Symptoms of Uncontrolled High Blood Pressure



  • Headaches

  • Dizziness

  • Shortness of breath

  • Chest pain

  • Fatigue

  • Blurred vision

  • Nosebleeds (in some cases)

Common Causes of High Blood Pressure (Hypertension)


1. Unhealthy Diet


  • Excessive salt (sodium) intake

  • High consumption of processed and fast foods

  • Diets high in saturated and trans fats

2. Lack of Physical Activity


  • Sedentary lifestyle

  • Insufficient exercise leading to weight gain and poor cardiovascular health

3. Obesity and Overweight


  • Excess body weight increases the workload on the heart and blood vessels.

4. Smoking and Tobacco Use


  • Nicotine narrows blood vessels and raises blood pressure.

Ayurvedic Treatment for BP


In Ayurveda, blood pressure disorders are associated with an imbalance of the Vata, Pitta, and Kapha doshas, along with factors such as stress, improper diet, sedentary lifestyle, and impaired circulation. Ayurvedic treatment aims to restore balance, support heart health, improve circulation, and promote mental calmness.

  • Abhyanga (Oil Massage) – Warm herbal oil massage that promotes relaxation and improves circulation.

3. Diet and Lifestyle Recommendations


  • Reduce excessive salt intake.

  • Eat fresh fruits and vegetables.

  • Include whole grains and fiber-rich foods.

  • Avoid processed, fried, and overly salty foods.

  • Maintain a healthy body weight.

  • Exercise regularly through walking, yoga, or light physical activity.

  • Practice meditation and stress-management techniques.

  • Get adequate sleep and maintain a regular routine.
