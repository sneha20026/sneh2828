Know More About Thyroid Disease


Thyroid disease refers to disorders affecting the thyroid gland, a small butterfly-shaped gland located at the front of the neck. The thyroid produces hormones that regulate metabolism, energy levels, body temperature, heart rate, growth, and many other essential body functions.
When the thyroid gland produces too much or too little hormone, it can lead to various health problems collectively known as thyroid disorders.

Types of Thyroid Disease


1. Hypothyroidism


Occurs when the thyroid gland does not produce enough thyroid hormones.

Common Symptoms:

  • Fatigue

  • Weight gain

  • Dry skin

  • Hair loss

  • Constipation

  • Depression

  • Sensitivity to cold

2. Hyperthyroidism


Occurs when the thyroid gland produces excessive thyroid hormones.

Common Symptoms:

  • Weight loss

  • Rapid heartbeat

  • Anxiety and irritability

  • Excessive sweating

  • Tremors

  • Increased appetite

  • Difficulty sleeping

3. Goiter


An abnormal enlargement of the thyroid gland that may occur with normal, low, or high thyroid hormone levels.

4. Hashimoto's Thyroiditis


An autoimmune condition in which the immune system attacks the thyroid gland, often leading to hypothyroidism.

5. Grave's Disease


An autoimmune disorder and one of the most common causes of hyperthyroidism.

Common Causes of Thyroid Disease


  • Autoimmune disorders

  • Iodine deficiency or excess

  • Genetic factors

  • Thyroid inflammation

  • Certain medications

  • Pregnancy-related hormonal changes

  • Radiation treatment affecting the neck
